# Curriculum evidence and scope

Reference read on 12 October 2026: **Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia, KSSM Bahasa Inggeris Tingkatan 5, November 2020**, published by Bahagian Pembangunan Kurikulum. [Mirrored original PDF](https://alia.samsulzamzuri.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/DSKP-KSSM-FORM-5.pdf). This is a ministry-authored document on a third-party mirror, not an official KPM hosting domain.

Printed p. 2 gives secondary CEFR progression; the Form 5 target is B1 High. Printed pp. 43–44 describe Form 5 grammar and recycling. Future perfect forms appear in the Form 5 list. The appendices, printed pp. 50–51, provide earlier-form grammar context. Grammar is connected to communicative use and the four language skills.

The prototype uses these as context, not as evidence that every topic is compulsory at every form. Its Form 1–5 filters are teacher-facing revision/extension recommendations. More complex perfect-progressive topics are allocated conservatively to later secondary contexts. No specific content-standard or learning-standard code is invented. Form 5 extension tags are distinguished from recommendations in the typed mapping data.

Additional school document collection: [AKSES English Panel: syllabus, DSKP and KSSM](https://sites.google.com/moe-dl.edu.my/aksesenglishpanel/home/resources/sukatan-dskp-kssm). It lists form-specific resources; it is an entry point for further source verification, not proof of detailed topic placement.

Before Stage 2, collect and verify the school’s current Form 1–5 DSKP and scheme-of-work versions. An experienced Malaysian English teacher should review local lesson placement and Bahasa Melayu terminology. The original explanations, examples and activities in this prototype are not copied from textbook passages.
