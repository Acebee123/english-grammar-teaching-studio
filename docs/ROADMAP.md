# Phased roadmap

Stage 0: establish schema, content/services boundaries, light professional canvas, local persistence, packaging and export interfaces. Implemented.

Stage 1: functional Tenses & Aspects prototype. Implemented for teacher review, with verification and limitations recorded separately. Do not fill the other modules with shallow sample content to claim completion.

Next release gate: review the prototype with a Malaysian secondary English teacher, verify bilingual terminology, test on the intended classroom projector and review exported materials. Resolve review findings before expansion.

Stage 2: Sentence Structure & Parts of Speech; Subject–Verb Agreement. Add broader reviewed POS/phrase structures and British collective-noun variants. Preserve uncertainty around ambiguous sentences.

Stage 3: Active & Passive Voice; Direct & Reported Speech. Add argument-structure checks, semantic constraints and contexts where backshift is unnecessary.

Stage 4: Conditionals, Modals, Complex Sentences & Clauses. Add mixed time references, modal ambiguity and explicit clause relationships.

Stage 5: cross-module content/editorial review, classroom pilot, accessibility review, import migrations, provider integration checks and distribution regression tests. No expansion is authorised as part of this delivery beyond the first module’s architecture.
